At Nearby Friends, we respect your right to privacy and control over your personal data.

How to Delete Your Data

Option 1: In-App Deletion (Recommended)

The fastest way to delete your data is through the app:

  1. Open the Nearby Friends app.
  2. Go to the Profile screen.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the permanent deletion.

This will immediately and permanently delete your profile, location history, and friend connections from our servers.

Option 2: Manual Request

If you have already uninstalled the app and wish to request data deletion manually, please email us from the email address associated with your account.

Email: pounkumar.p@gmail.com
Subject: Data Deletion Request - [Your Phone Number]

We will process your request and remove all your data within 48 hours.

What data is deleted?

When you delete your account, the following data is wiped permanently:

  • Your Name, Email, and Phone Number.
  • Your current and historical location coordinates.
  • Your friend list and follow requests.
  • Your saved location reminders.
