Privacy Policy

Last Updated: Jun 1, 2026

Welcome to Nearby Friends. Your privacy is our top priority. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

Important: Nearby Friends is a location-sharing app. By using it, you are choosing to share your location with friends you have explicitly approved.

1. Information We Collect

  • Precise Location Data: We collect precise location data to show you on your friends' maps and send proximity alerts. This tracking remains active via a persistent notification when the app is in the background.
  • Contacts: We access your contact list to identify which of your friends are already using the app. This processing happens locally and matches phone numbers to our user database.
  • Personal Information: We collect your name, email address, and phone number for account creation and authentication.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected data for the following purposes:

  • To provide core app functionality (showing friends on a map).
  • To notify you when a friend is nearby.
  • To allow you to set location-based reminders.
  • To manage your account and verify your identity.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Your location is shared ONLY with users you have accepted as "Friends" within the app. You can stop sharing your location at any time by turning off the "Persistent Tracking" toggle in the app settings.

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption (TLS/HTTPS) to protect your data in transit. All user location data is stored securely using Firebase (Google Cloud).

5. User Rights & Deletion

You have the right to access, update, or delete your information at any time. You can delete your account and all associated data directly within the Profile screen of the app.
